## Electrical and water emergencies
For a downed power line, sparks, an electrical fire, a flooded electrical panel or a water main break, call 911 if anyone is in danger, then the HCU 24-hour emergency line at 1-800-555-0199. Emergency calls are answered by a person 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

## Gas smell
Harbor City Utilities provides electricity and water, not gas. If you smell gas, leave the building right away without using switches or phones inside, then call 911 and your gas company from outside.

## Medical emergencies during an outage
If someone needs power for medical equipment and the power is out, call 911 if they are in danger. Then call the emergency line so we can check your area's restoration time.

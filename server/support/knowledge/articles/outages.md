## Reporting an outage
You can report a power outage through Clara in chat, online, or by calling 1-800-555-0142. First check your circuit breakers or fuses. If your neighbours also have no power, it is probably an outage in the area.

## Restoration times
When you report an outage, we show an estimated restoration time. Most outages are fixed within 4 hours, but storms can take longer. We restore power to hospitals and emergency services first, then to the largest number of customers at once.

## Medical equipment and priority restoration
If someone in your home uses life-support or medical equipment that needs electricity, you can register for priority restoration and outage call-backs. During a long outage, always have a backup plan, because priority restoration cannot be guaranteed.

## Downed power lines
Stay at least 35 feet (10 metres) away from any downed power line, and assume it is live. Call 911 and the HCU 24-hour emergency line at 1-800-555-0199. Never try to move a line yourself.

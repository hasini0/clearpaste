## What each line on your bill means
Your Harbor City Utilities bill has a customer charge, energy and delivery charges for electricity, and usage charges for water and sewer. The customer charge is a flat $12.50 per month for electric service and covers meters, billing and customer service, even if you use no power. The energy charge is $0.142 per kWh and pays for the electricity you used. The delivery charge is $0.061 per kWh and pays for the poles, wires and substations that bring power to your home.

## Water and sewer charges
Water is billed at $4.85 per CCF. One CCF is 100 cubic feet, which is about 748 gallons. Sewer charges are based on your winter water use, so watering your garden in summer does not raise your sewer charge. A 6% city utility tax is added to the total, as required by Harbor City.

## Due date and late fees
Your bill is due 21 days after the bill date printed at the top. If a payment is late, a late fee of 1.5% of the past-due balance is added, with a minimum of $5. The "past-due balance" is money from earlier bills that has not been paid yet; the "current charges" are this month's new charges.

## Estimated readings
If your bill shows the letter "E" next to the meter reading, your usage was estimated because we could not read your meter. Estimates are corrected automatically on your next actual reading. You can also send a photo of your meter through chat or the website to get a corrected bill.

## What a payment plan is
A Deferred Payment Arrangement (payment plan) lets you split your past-due balance into equal monthly installments over 3, 6 or 12 months. Each installment is added to your regular monthly bill. Setting up a payment plan stops any pending disconnection while you keep to the plan.

## Who can get a payment plan
You can set up a payment plan if your past-due balance is at least $50 and you have not broken a payment plan in the last 12 months. If you broke a plan in the last 12 months, a customer care specialist can still review your situation.

## Down payment
A down payment of 10% of the past-due balance is due when the plan starts. The down payment is waived for customers who receive HELP Fund assistance or the Senior and Disability Discount.

## Keeping the plan
To keep the plan, pay each monthly bill, including the installment, by its due date. If you miss a payment, contact us before the next due date; one missed installment can usually be caught up without cancelling the plan.

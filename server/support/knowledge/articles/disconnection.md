## When we send a disconnection notice
Harbor City Utilities only disconnects service after a balance is more than 30 days past due and at least $100. We always mail a written 10-day disconnection notice first. We never disconnect on a Friday, a weekend, a holiday, or the day before a holiday.

## How to stop a disconnection
You can stop a disconnection by paying the past-due amount, by setting up a payment plan, by applying for the HELP Fund, or by sending a medical certificate. Any of these, done before the date on your notice, stops the shutoff.

## Medical certificate
If a member of your household has a serious medical condition, or uses medical equipment that needs power, a doctor or nurse can complete a medical certificate. It holds off disconnection for 60 days and can be renewed twice in a 12-month period. You still owe the balance, so we recommend also setting up a payment plan.

## Winter protection
From November 15 to March 15, we do not disconnect residential customers who are enrolled in an assistance program, and we do not disconnect anyone on a day when the forecast low is below 32°F (0°C).

## Reconnection
If your service was disconnected, it is reconnected the same day when payment or a plan is in place before 3pm. The reconnection fee is $35, and it is waived for customers in an assistance program.

## Language help
Clara can answer in 30 languages in chat. By phone, interpreters are available in more than 200 languages at no cost: call 1-800-555-0142 and say the name of your language.

## Accessible bills
We offer large-print and Braille bills, and bills read aloud by phone. Customers who are deaf or hard of hearing can reach us through relay service by dialling 711.

## Someone to help you manage your account
You can name a trusted person, such as a family member or caregiver, as a third-party contact. They receive a copy of any disconnection notice, so a missed letter never leads to a shutoff.

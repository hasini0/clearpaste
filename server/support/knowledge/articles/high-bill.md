## Common reasons for a high bill
The most common reasons for a higher bill are: colder or hotter weather (heating and air conditioning use the most energy), an estimated meter reading, more days in the billing period, a past-due balance carried from an earlier bill, or a water leak. Space heaters are a frequent cause in winter: one 1,500-watt heater running 8 hours a day adds about $55 a month.

## Checking for a water leak
To check for a water leak, turn off every tap and appliance that uses water, then look at your water meter. If the small dial or triangle on the meter is still moving, water is leaking somewhere. A running toilet can waste 200 gallons a day. If you find and fix a leak, you may qualify for a leak adjustment.

## Leak adjustments
Harbor City Utilities gives a leak adjustment once every 12 months. We credit 50% of the water used above your normal average, for up to two billing periods. You need to send proof of repair, such as a plumber's invoice or a receipt for parts, within 60 days of the high bill.

## If the reading looks wrong
If you think your meter was read wrong, compare the reading on your bill with the number on your meter. If the bill says "E" (estimated), send us a meter photo and we will correct the bill. You can also ask for a free meter test once every 24 months.

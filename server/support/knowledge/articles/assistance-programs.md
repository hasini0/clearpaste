## HELP Fund
The HELP Fund gives a one-time credit of up to $300 on your bill, once every 12 months. To qualify, your household income must be at or below 200% of the federal poverty guideline, and you must have a past-due balance or a disconnection notice. For 2026 that limit is $31,300 a year for a household of 1, $42,300 for 2, $53,300 for 3 and $64,300 for 4, plus $11,000 for each extra person.

## Senior and Disability Discount
The Senior and Disability Discount takes 20% off your monthly charges. You qualify if someone on the account is 65 or older or receives disability benefits, and household income is at or below 250% of the federal poverty guideline.

## Energy assistance from the state (LIHEAP)
The Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program (LIHEAP) is run by the state and can pay part of your heating bill. Clara can give you a referral, and applications are made through your local community action agency.

## How to apply
You can apply for HCU programs through Clara in chat, online, or by phone. You will need proof of household income, such as pay stubs, a benefits letter or a tax return. Applications are usually reviewed within 5 business days, and any pending disconnection is paused while we review.

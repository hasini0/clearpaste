## How Harbor City Utilities really contacts you
Harbor City Utilities never calls, texts or emails to demand immediate payment to avoid a shutoff within the hour. We always mail a written 10-day disconnection notice first. We never ask for payment by gift card, cryptocurrency, wire transfer or a payment app sent to a person, and we never ask for your full card number or password by text.

## Signs of a utility scam
Common signs of a scam are: a threat to disconnect today, a demand to pay with gift cards or crypto, a caller who asks you to stay on the line while you buy cards, a text with a short or unusual link, and a "refund" you did not expect.

## What to do if you are not sure
Hang up or do not reply. Call the number printed on your bill, 1-800-555-0142, or paste the message into chat and Clara will check it for scam warning signs. If you already paid a scammer, call your bank right away and report it to the police non-emergency line.

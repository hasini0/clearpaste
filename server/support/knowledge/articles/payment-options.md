## Free ways to pay
You can pay for free online in the customer portal, by setting up AutoPay, or by phone through our automated line at 1-800-555-0142, 24 hours a day. You can also mail a check to the address on your bill; allow 5 to 7 days for it to arrive.

## Paying in person
You can pay cash at partner stores displaying the HCU Pay sign. Partner stores charge a $1.50 convenience fee, and the payment posts to your account within one hour.

## AutoPay and paperless billing
AutoPay takes your payment automatically on the due date, so you are never late. If you sign up for both AutoPay and paperless billing, you get a $2 credit on every bill.

## Payments we never accept
Harbor City Utilities never accepts payment by gift card, cryptocurrency, wire transfer or payment apps sent to a person. Anyone asking you to pay that way is not from HCU.

## How much notice to give
Please give at least 3 business days' notice to start, stop or move service. You can schedule it up to 30 days ahead. Service stops at your old address and starts at your new address on the dates you choose, so you are never without power or water on moving day.

## Starting new service
To start service you need a government photo ID and the date you will move in. A deposit of up to two times your average monthly bill may be required if you do not have a credit history with us. The deposit is waived if you have had 12 months of on-time payments with HCU.

## Final bill
When you stop service, we read the meter on your stop date and send a final bill within 10 days to the forwarding address you give us.

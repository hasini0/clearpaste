## Talking to a person
You can chat with a customer care specialist in this chat, ask for a call-back at a time that suits you, send an email to help@harborcity-utilities.example, or call 1-800-555-0142. Customer care is open Monday to Friday 7am to 8pm, and Saturday 8am to 2pm. Emergencies are answered 24 hours a day at 1-800-555-0199.
